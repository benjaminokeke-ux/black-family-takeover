Seabrook has more than 300 homes in its vacation rental program, all managed by Seabrook Hospitality, from two-person carriage homes to houses that sleep 20 or more. Everything is walkable: the beach, the pools, the restaurants and the town green. That makes it easy for a large group to spread across many houses and still be together all week.

We'll reserve one large "hub" house for group meals and evenings. Your family books its own home nearby, on your own card, under Seabrook's standard terms.

What's a founding family?

Founding families are the first households to commit. They lock in the core of the Takeover early, so the week has a group to build around while the best homes near the hub are still open.

  • Book by Dec 15, 2026. Founding families either take rooms in the hub house or book their own homes alongside it. Seabrook charges a 50% deposit at booking, fully refunded if you cancel 30 or more days before arrival.
  • First pick of hub house rooms and of homes near the hub.
  • Help shape the week: the events, the hub house and who runs what.
  • Five spots at most. So the hub house doesn't get crowded, the founding option on the form closes once it's full.

Not ready to commit that early? No problem. Everyone else books as early as they're comfortable, and no later than early spring.

The week

A draft schedule. Daytime activities and evening gatherings, everything optional, plenty of beach time in between.

Sat, Aug 7

Arrival + opening cookout

Check in from 4 PM, then grill at the hub house.

Sun, Aug 8

Beach yoga + Okeke Family Pizza Party

Morning flow on the sand led by Nakita Manning of Focus & Flow Collective, all levels. In the evening, the Okekes host pizza night and teach everyone Mau Mau, the family card game, so you can play it all week.

Mon, Aug 9

Golf outing + group bike ride, then wine tasting

Ocean Shores Golf Course, a public 18-hole course about 25 minutes south. Non-golfers ride Seabrook's trails; Basecamp in town rents bikes. Evening wine tasting for the grown-ups.

Tue, Aug 10

BBQ cookoff + ice cream social

House against house. Bring your rub, your sauce and your trash talk. Everyone else judges, then we cool off with an ice cream social for all ages.

Wed, Aug 11

Game night + beach bonfire with s'mores

Tables for spades, dominoes, Uno, Mau Mau and Monopoly. Then down to the beach for a bonfire and s'mores; Seabrook keeps its community fire pits stocked with wood.

Thu, Aug 12

Illumination Night + whiskey stations

Our own nod to Oak Bluffs: lanterns and string lights on every Takeover porch and a walk through town to see them all. Along the way, a few host homes each pour a different whiskey for the grown-ups to try.

Fri, Aug 13

Closing yoga, farewell dinner + karaoke

One more flow with Nakita, then a closing dinner and a karaoke send-off. Seabrook usually hosts free Friday sunset concerts through August; the 2027 lineup isn't out yet.

Sat, Aug 14

Checkout by 11 AM

Also on offer any day: clamming when the razor clam tides open, pickleball and cornhole at the pool house, kids' beach games, and day trips to Ocean Shores, about 25 minutes south.

Why this week

August is the warmest month on this coast. Aug 7–14 also ends before Seattle Prep and Seattle Academy start orientation, classes or fall sports, based on their 2026 calendars. Neither school has published its 2027 dates yet.

Budget rule of thumb

Take a home's listed weekly total and add about 12% for lodging tax. A typical 3-bedroom comes to roughly $7,650 for the week. Split between two families, that's about $3,800 each.

Getting there

By car
About 2½ hours from Seattle, about 3 hours from Portland. No mountain passes or ferries.
Fly into
Sea-Tac (SEA) is the main airport
Airport shuttle
Seabrook runs a shuttle to and from Sea-Tac. Book through the Concierge.
Small airport
Bowerman (HQM) in Hoquiam is 28 miles away. Shuttle is $45 per person each way.

For maps, use 4275 SR 109, Pacific Beach, WA 98571. Seabrook Concierge: (360) 276-4108 · concierge@seabrookwa.com. The Sea-Tac shuttle typically leaves Seabrook early morning and returns early afternoon, so confirm times before booking flights.

What to expect

Weather

August highs are usually around 65°F, rarely below 60 or above 73. The ocean is cold. Bring layers.

In town

Indoor and outdoor pools, fitness center, walk-on beach access, shops, restaurants and a market.

Free gear

Guests borrow cornhole, pickleball, bocce, croquet, spikeball, footballs and more from the pool house.

Phones

Verizon and AT&T work best. Every home has Wi-Fi for calling.

Homes include

Made beds, bath and beach towels for everyone, kitchen basics, starter coffee, dish and laundry soap, TV and Wi-Fi. Bring spices, oil, condiments, food storage and any baby gear. Cribs rent for $40.

Good to know

  • Clamming needs a Washington license, sold in Pacific Beach. Seabrook's beach follows the Mocrocks razor clam tides.
  • The nearest hospital is about 45 minutes away in Aberdeen.
  • Portable fire pits aren't allowed at homes. Use the community fire pits.
